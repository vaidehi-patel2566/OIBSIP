Sources. WHO, Global status report on drowning prevention, 2024: 300,000 drowning deaths in 2021; more than 30 deaths an hour; the death rate fell 38% from 2000, from 6.1 to 3.8 per 100,000; the European Region fell 68% and the African Region 3%; 24% of deaths (73,000) were children under five. WHO, Drowning fact sheet: 92% of drowning deaths occur in low- and middle-income countries.
