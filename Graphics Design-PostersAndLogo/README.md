# Graphics Design — Posters and Logo

Graphics for the fictional dawn swim club Vela, plus two posters from the same brief. Tide is `#0E4D5C`, sunrise amber is `#E7A15A`, and the paper is `#F4F1E8`. Headings are Syne. Body type is Outfit.

## Task 1 — Posters

| Poster | Theme | File |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harbor Lights | One-night waterfront jazz concert | `posters/harbor-lights.png`, `posters/harbor-lights.pdf` |
| Aero One | Wireless earbud product launch | `posters/aero-one.png`, `posters/aero-one.pdf` |

Both posters are A4 (210 × 297 mm). The PNGs are 2382 × 3369 px (about 288 dpi). The PDFs are single-page A4 with the type kept as vectors.

Harbor Lights uses three colours: navy `#102033`, cream `#F4EBDD`, amber `#E3A008`. The custom graphic is a pier, a string of lights, and a cropped moon. Aero One uses paper `#F4F1E8`, ink `#141414`, and lime `#D6F34E`. The custom graphic is a geometric earbud with sound arcs.

Hierarchy on each poster is headline, subheadline, body copy, then a button CTA.

Contrast, checked against WCAG:

- Cream on navy, and navy on cream: 13.9:1
- Amber on navy, and navy on the amber button: 7.3:1
- Ink on paper, and paper on ink: 16.3:1
- Lime on ink, and ink on the lime button: 14.7:1

All of these clear the 4.5:1 threshold for body text.

## Task 2 — Logo

Vela Swim Club. The source files are vector SVG. The PNGs are 2000 × 2000 px.

| Variant | File |
| --- | --- |
| Full colour, transparent background | `logo/vela-color.svg`, `logo/vela-color.png` |
| Monochrome black, transparent background | `logo/vela-mono.svg`, `logo/vela-mono.png` |
| Reversed, white on tide blue | `logo/vela-reversed.svg`, `logo/vela-reversed.png` |

The mono PNG is black artwork on a transparent background, so it disappears on a dark preview. Open the SVG, or place the PNG on white.

Tide `#0E4D5C` on white is 9.4:1. White on tide `#0E4D5C` is 9.4:1.

Type is Syne for VELA and Outfit for SWIM CLUB. Fraunces is used for the Harbor Lights headlines. All three faces are from Google Fonts and are included under the SIL Open Font License in `fonts/`.

## Task 3 — Business card

Mara Ellison, Founder of Vela Swim Club. The print file is `business-card/vela-card.pdf` (two pages). Each page is 91 × 61 mm, which is the 85 × 55 mm card plus 3 mm bleed on every side. `business-card/vela-card-preview.png` shows the front and the back.

The front carries the logo, her name, and the title. The back carries the phone, email, website, and Instagram handle. Type is Syne and Outfit only.

## Task 4 — Infographic

`infographic/swimming-counted.png` is 800 × 2000 px at 150 dpi. `infographic/swimming-counted@2x.png` is the same layout at 1600 × 4000 px, 300 dpi. The piece is a top-to-bottom briefing with five sections: a toll, a rate comparison, a participation bar chart, a regional comparison, and a 92% ring. Icons, bars, and the ring are the three kinds of graphic. The colours are the Vela tide, amber, paper, and white. Every figure is sourced in the footer from the WHO Global status report on drowning prevention (2024), the WHO drowning fact sheet, and Sport England’s Active Lives Adult Survey.

## Task 5 — Journal

`magazine/vela-journal.pdf` is a four-page A4 issue, First Light. Every page has the same running header and a page number. The two text pages use a two-column grid. Body copy is Outfit at 11 pt with 1.5 line spacing. Page 2 has a pull quote. Pages 1 and 3 are full-bleed photographs from Unsplash (`magazine/images/cover.jpg`, `magazine/images/coast.jpg`).

## LinkedIn post

Two print posters and a logo from a graphics brief.

Harbor Lights is a one-night jazz poster in navy, cream, and amber: a pier, a string of lights, and a cropped moon, with the date and a reserve button set apart from the headline. Aero One is an earbud launch in paper, ink, and lime, built around a geometric earbud and a single pre-order button.

Vela is a dawn swim club. The mark is a sail and a sun on one horizon line — vela is Latin for sail — with tide blue for the water and sunrise amber used only on the sun, so the symbol stays simple. The wordmark is Syne, tracked open, with Outfit in a smaller line for “Swim Club.” The same mark and pair of typefaces carry the business card, the Counted infographic, and issue 01 of Vela Journal.
